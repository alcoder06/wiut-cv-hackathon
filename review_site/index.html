Redraw the zones

  1. Zoom to 2× or 4× and scroll to the crossing. Pick a frame where cars don't hide the stripes.
  2. Click a crossing in the list, then drag its corners so the outline hugs the painted stripes from kerb to kerb. Stop at an island; don't include it.
  3. Drag a small white dot to add a corner. Right-click a corner to remove it.
  4. For each island people wait on, press Add safe zone, click its corners on the picture, then press Enter.
  5. Press Save zones.

Real and not real are the team's majority; a tie between real and not real is a split, and splits stay out of the answer key. How this is used: every answer here becomes dev/labels.json in the organizers' ground-truth format. scripts/tune.py then picks each rule's thresholds and start/end shifts by the official metric against it. The candidates include what looser and stricter settings would report, so tuning is judged on events someone has actually looked at.

Score A averages F1 over event types, and an event type we report that never happens in the test set scores zero. A type with no real events here is a candidate to switch off.
